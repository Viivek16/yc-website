# YELLOW CAPITAL - MASTER DESIGN SYSTEM

## 1. Brand Identity & Aesthetic
- **Vibe:** "Institutional Market-Making meets Premium Web3 Venture Capital." (Minimalist, sharp, technical, and calm).
- **Backgrounds:** Pitch black (`#000000`). Incorporate abstract, thin-line geometric grids and interconnected data nodes. No noisy particle effects or 3D coins.
- **Animations:** Purpose-driven. Smooth scroll-reveals and snappy hover states.

## 2. Color Palette (Strict Hex Codes)
- **Primary Base (Background):** `#000000` (Deep Black)
- **Surface (Cards):** `#333333` (Dark Gray)
- **Borders/Dividers:** `#666666` (Mid Gray)
- **Primary Text:** `#FFFFFF` (White)
- **Secondary Text:** `#999999` (Light Gray)
- **Primary Accent (CTAs, Active States):** `#FCD000` (Vibrant Yellow)
- **Secondary Accent (Hover States):** `#F8C200` (Muted Yellow)

## 3. Typography
- **Headings (`h1` - `h6`):** `Gilmer` (sans-serif). Use for high contrast, confident, tight tracking.
- **Body & Labels (`p`, `span`):** `Metro` (sans-serif). Clean, highly readable.
- **Official/Quotes:** `Merriweather` (serif). Use for case studies and institutional authority.

## 4. UI Components
- **Buttons:** Primary buttons are `#FCD000` background with `#000000` text. Hover to `#F8C200`.
